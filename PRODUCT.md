# Soft Skills Lab

## Platform
web

## Stack
SvelteKit with SSR on Cloudflare Workers.

## Users
Parents in Akko (Acre), Israel, Russian-speaking, choosing an after-school group for a child aged 6–16 — often looking at the page together with the child. Includes active kids and kids with ADHD.

## Product Purpose
A year-long course of mini-groups (max 6 children) where real science experiments (physics, chemistry, engineering challenges, Rube Goldberg chain reactions) are used as metaphors and practice for soft skills: communication, cooperation, conflict resolution, emotional self-regulation, critical thinking, resilience. Adlerian psychology of equality; no grades, no lectures.

## Positioning
Not a science club and not a psychologist's office: a group where kids learn to talk, listen and agree with each other *through* doing experiments together. The group itself (from "Я" to "МЫ") is the point.

## Product Principles
- Show the group, not the lone child.
- Every experiment maps to a social skill, stated plainly.
- Warm, curious, never clinical; parents must trust it, kids must want it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
