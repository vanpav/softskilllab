# Brand: Soft Skills Lab

Chosen on 2026-11-06 after six rounds of logo exploration. This file records the decision and the rules. `DESIGN.md` still owns the page's visual system; `PRODUCT.md` owns audience and facts.

## Identity

- Name: Soft Skills Lab (Russian: Лаборатория Soft Skills). Write "Soft Skills Lab" in Latin everywhere the logo appears.
- Slogan: «Экспериментируй, познавай, расти». Caveat 700, pen blue. It is not part of the logo files; set it beside or under the logo when a layout has room.
- Mark: two flasks clinking, a lime marker patch behind them, a pink burst above. It stands for the group: two flasks meeting, not a lone child. The old flask-and-profile logo and the mascot are not part of this identity.

## Mark

- ViewBox 260 × 230. Flask path `M-12-118H12V-74C12-62 18-54 26-42L40-22C52-4 48 0 28 0H-28C-48 0-52-4-40-22L-26-42C-18-54-12-62-12-74Z`, rounded on purpose: no sharp tips, no wobble filter.
- Left flask `translate(72 205) rotate(24)`, right flask `translate(188 205) rotate(-24)`. White fill, ink line 9, round joins. Liquid levels stay level: pen blue left, pale pink right.
- Marker patch: three strokes, width 58, round caps, rotated −9°, shifted (9, 8). It sticks out under the blue flask and stays tidy under the pink one.
- Flasks and burst sit 11.6 units above the patch.
- Burst: three pink rays, line 8.
- On dark: no halo and no extra outline. Known weakness: the ink outline merges with the dark background where it leaves the lime patch. Alternative not yet tried: light outline and dark flasks on dark.
- One colour: pen blue, no patch, flask fill transparent.

## Favicon

- One upright flask with pen-blue liquid and an ink line on a square lime tile: `static/favicon.svg`, plus `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48) and `apple-touch-icon.png` (180, without rounded corners).
- It departs from the two-flask mark on purpose. Tried at 16 px, two flasks in any tilt merged into a tent or a butterfly, and the burst and marker patch disappeared.

## Wordmark

- Onest 900, tracking −0.025em, outlined in the SVGs (no kerning applied).
- Colour: ink on light, white on dark, pen blue in mono.
- "Lab" sits on an organic lime blob rotated −3° (`border-radius: 48% 52% 46% 54% / 62% 46% 54% 40%`, padding .22em). Text on the blob: pen blue on light, ink on dark. Mono: outlined blob, no fill.

## Lockups

- Mark to text size: at mark width 230 px the font is about 66 px (mark width is roughly 3.5× the font size). A size ratio of 0.85× or 1.15× was tried and rejected as worse.
- Visible gap between the flask edge and the text: 12 px at mark width 230 px (scale proportionally).
- The mark's visual centre aligns with the centre of the text block, slogan excluded. The slogan hangs below the block and does not move the mark.
- Two arrangements only: three lines (Soft / Skills / Lab) for square places and the footer, one line for the header and wide places. Rejected: "Живое Lab" (handwritten Lab with bubbles), "Пузырящиеся буквы", "Штамп" (framed).
- In the one-line lockup "Lab" tucks closer to "Skills" by one space width; the slogan sits 8 px closer than the stacked layout.
